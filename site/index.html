SHS Atlas BETA

A 3D map of Stevenson High School with every room, plus walking directions from any room to any other room. Made by a student, not by the school.

Heads up, this is a beta!

It might be slow right now, especially on older phones, and some things are still being fixed. If SHS Atlas goes public it will get a login (security sign-in) so only Stevenson people can use it.

Continue to the map →

Works best on a phone or iPad. One finger moves, two fingers zoom and turn, three fingers tilt.

Version history

  • v0.1 CURRENT (Oct 28, 2026): Doors are marked with gold frames, and rooms that were missing a door have one. Windows in outside walls are filled in. Hallways are smoother and reach the walls. The PWC atrium stairs are real walkable steps up to level 2. Storage rooms and small offices show just a small number so the map is less cluttered. Three fingers tilt the view. Beta password lock.
  • v0.0.8 (Oct 28, 2026): Much faster and stops crashing on phones. New touch controls like a maps app. Directions now start from straight above you, facing where you're going. Fixed the flickering ground around the stadium.
  • v0.0.7: School colors and the Patriots logo. Red route line. Walls are darker so you can tell where you can't walk.
  • v0.0.6: Brand new look: search bar on top, floor buttons on the side, swipe-up panel, dark mode.
  • v0.0.5: Walking directions between any two rooms, an elevators-only option, Plan my day, and three ways into the pool (both locker rooms, plus the deck entrance after 3:30).
  • v0.0.4: Level 3 added from the district campus map and checked against door photos. Level 4 (the rooftop greenhouse) added.
  • v0.0.3: West Building levels 1 and 2 redone from the campus map. Hallways and walls marked.
  • v0.0.2: Rooms traced from the official floor plans for East levels 1–2 and the Link.
  • v0.0.1: First try: just the outside of the campus (buildings, parking lots, fields).

GitHub

Project info and bug reports: github.com/banxxrr/SHS-Atlas-Info

The code itself is kept private so the sign-in planned for later can't be skipped by downloading it.

SHS Atlas is not an official Stevenson High School or District 125 website. Room info comes from the district's 2023–24 campus map and floor plans and might have mistakes. Found one? Tap the room on the map and choose Edit label.
